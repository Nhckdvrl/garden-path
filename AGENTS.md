## 2026-10-12 user methodology update: do not over-police novelty or data

- Human psycholinguistics papers from 2006 or earlier **precede modern LLMs**. Carefully transferring a known human phenomenon to a modern LLM, and documenting whether it occurs and when, can itself be scientifically novel. Generic LM priming is not automatically the same as Garden Path *misparse-specific* priming. Read [scientific novelty standard](research/SCIENTIFIC_NOVELTY_STANDARD.md) before deciding an idea lacks value. No mandatory one-mechanism/three-architecture prerequisite for a pilot.
- Data are central scientific material, not disposable evaluation scaffolding. Read [data strategy](research/DATA_STRATEGY.md); prefer established published stimuli. Count INDEPENDENT lexical families, not repeated questions/targets/model runs. Sample size follows study effect/uncertainty and prior literature, not a universal 20–30 items.
- Natural problems deserve natural data. Use sourced corpus and audited derived variants before synthetic templates. When published data lack key labels/controls, edit/add only what the question requires, keep originals and source/license/revision hashes.
- OpenCode **currently free** models may help propose/review crucial data and a risk-stratified random sample; free status may change and client authentication is external. Confirm explicit zero charge and privacy prior to use, never silently fall back to paid, never treat one model's judgment as gold. [data-assisted protocol](research/DATA_ASSISTED_REVIEW.md).
- Avoid excessive audit bureaucracy: automatic checks for all, targeted review for suspicious or changed material, stratified random QC for untouched published material, independent verification for *new critical semantics*, escalate uncertainty. Never discard samples because the tested model failed.
