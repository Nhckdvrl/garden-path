# Ceháková & Chromý (2023) — Garden-path sentences and the diversity of their (mis)representations

[PLOS ONE primary open full article](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0288817); data and R analysis [OSF](https://osf.io/98g26/). **READ SCOPE: publisher abstract, rationale, method/result excerpts and General Discussion ``Experiment2/3/5 and final section checked; full tables/appendices not systematically reread.**

## How the idea grew (RECONSTRUCTED)

- **Parent controversy:** lingering initial misinterpretation (Christianson) can coexist with successful reanalysis of the real structure (Slattery). Chromý 2022 instead showed globally incoherent sentence results on difficult Czech constructions.
- **Pressure:** A binary 'correct parse established / not established' story may be too simple; different question formats prompt different local associations.
- **Changed premise:** Instead of assuming a single final representation, test **its diversity along two axes**: fidelity to the input vs coherence across local relationships.
- **Critical experiment sequence:** Czech patient/beneficiary ambiguity; sentence-at-once vs word-by-word self-paced reading × polar Yes/No vs open-ended questions; animate vs inanimate potential patient manipulation assesses semantic interference. No single answer format is the unique reading diagnostic.
- **Empirical message:** Early wrong meaning often lingers; participants frequently get some final interpretations right, yet findings for globally complete coherent representation are mixed. Some compatible local partial analyses or no coherent global representation persist, especially on complex items.
- **Experimental insight:** When 'Did the shopkeeper have a van?' is easy to guess by plausibility without parsing, a *free question 'Whose was the van?'* is more diagnostic of establishing the requested relation. The task must demand the specific relation, not just a plausible narrative continuation.
- **Scale:** 'Heterogeneity of *interpretations*' rather than only mean accuracy differences. This is direct precedent for our earlier “fragmentation” idea, so that broad claim is NOT ours.
- **Limits:** Differences in tasks/readout can drive differences in answers without providing a direct measurement of one internal global tree; some patterns weak or vary between experiments; human population and Czech case morphology differ from an English instruction LM.
- **Our gap if any:** Predict new **source-grounded dependency use** and *cross-task transfer* from specific structural conditions, beyond their already reported four experiments. Never claim a new two-way fidelity/coherence taxonomy.
