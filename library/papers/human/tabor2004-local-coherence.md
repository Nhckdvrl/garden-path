# Tabor, Galantucci & Richardson (2004) — Effects of merely local syntactic coherence on sentence processing

*JML* 50, 355–370. **Read scope: SEARCH-LEVEL ONLY** (secondary descriptions, 2026-10-13). The full text has not been read.

- **Finding:** in *The coach smiled at the player tossed a frisbee…*, the locally coherent substring *the player tossed a frisbee* (a main-clause reading that is globally impossible) slows reading compared with *thrown*. Readers are influenced by locally coherent structures that the global parse rules out.
- **Follow-ups:** Bicknell, Levy & Demberg 2009 (belief-update model, naturalistic text); Hancock & Tabor 2021 (self-organizing parsing). The search found no neural-LM study on these materials.
- **Use in this project:** motivated E011, which asks whether the LM production residue is local coherence (`V the N`) or a licensed object analysis. Answer: a licensed analysis (median ratio 0.10), with a possible configuration-level component in larger or instruction-tuned models.
