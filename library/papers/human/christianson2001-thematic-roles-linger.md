# Christianson, Hollingworth, Halliwell & Ferreira (2001) — Thematic roles assigned along the garden path linger

*Cognitive Psychology* 42, 368–407. doi:10.1006/cogp.2000.0752. **Read scope: FULL TEXT** (Ferreira-lab PDF, 2026-10-12), including both stimulus appendices, which are transcribed in `data/gp_behavior/upstream/christianson2001/`.

## Question and genesis

- **Field belief:** after a garden path, readers fully reanalyze. A correct final parse means the misanalysis is discarded.
- **DOCUMENTED pressure:** do readers fully revise the thematic roles assigned along the garden path, or do they leave *partial* reanalysis with the initial role still attached?
- **Design move:** ask about the obsolete relation (*Did the man hunt the deer?*) after GP vs non-GP controls, and manipulate the variables that predict incomplete revision.

## Experiments and key numbers (% incorrect "yes" to the obsolete-relation question)

| Exp | GP plaus short | GP plaus long | GP implaus short | GP implaus long | control short | control long |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1a (RSVP) | 44.0 | 51.2 | 20.2 | 43.7 | 21.4 | 31.0 |
| 1b (self-paced) | 28.6 | 43.7 | 11.1 | 31.4 | 10.7 | 16.3 |

- **Length** (*the deer that was brown and graceful*) increases lingering, consistent with digging in. **Plausibility** of the misreading increases it. The two did not interact in 1b.
- **Control** = *While the man hunted the pheasant the deer ran…* (V gets its own object, no comma).
- **Exp. 2:** clause order and head position.
- **Exp. 3a/3b:** reflexive-absolute-transitive (RAT) verbs (*bathe, dress, shave*) block the pragmatic inference to the object. GP 65.6% vs reordered control 12.5% (RAT). For optionally transitive verbs: 75.0% vs 49.0%. Syntactic lingering therefore survives where inference cannot explain it.

## What is owned / not owned

- **Owned:** lingering misinterpretation; length and plausibility effects; RAT dissociation of syntax from inference.
- **Not owned:** the relation between lingering and other residues (van Gompel 2006 priming), and anything about LMs.

## Use in this project

E003 uses all 54 families as published. LM results: lingering is large and length-sensitive (human-like). Plausibility acts as an additive prior in LMs (it is present even when the hunting is asserted). Lingering dissociates from priming item by item.
