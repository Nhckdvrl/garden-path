## A — Opportunity-conditioned preference evidence

**KILL — MAIN-SCALE INSUFFICIENCY, NOT EXACT COLLISION.**

Core:
same choice history + different feasible sets → different preference evidence.

Why killed:

- under the final-three standard, this is too small relative to the stronger surviving questions.

Do not keep as reserve merely because P0 material is ready.
