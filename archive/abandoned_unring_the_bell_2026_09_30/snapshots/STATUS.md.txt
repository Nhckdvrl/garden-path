# Current research status — 2026-09-30

**Latest research decision:** the [2026-09-30 reassessment](results/audits/project_reassessment_2026_09_30.md) supersedes the default next-experiment recommendation below. ConfB remains reliable empirical evidence, but no sufficiently new central claim has been established. G32 supports a model-bounded query effect, not a completed three-ability decomposition. Its record-only active-X cells also contain an admissible-set scope ambiguity. ConfB is a single rendered evidence-before-ruling prompt, with no initial admission instruction or actual earlier model judgment. Differences between ConfB and G32 therefore do not justify promoting eligibility history to the next mainline; G29's existing R/N findings must also constrain that proposal. The project is currently a **search lead with preserved empirical assets**, not a mainline awaiting one final experiment. No new inference is started by this reassessment.

The old prospective-exclusion paper framing remains superseded; original registrations and results remain unchanged. The [2026-09-28 research diagnosis](results/audits/research_diagnosis_2026_09_28.md) and [literature audit](results/audits/literature_diagnosis_2026_09_28.md) establish that a suppression–restoration gap **alone** is already close to Self-Blinding and cannot carry a novel Main-paper claim. G18's old headline was dropped because it was trivial, not because a clean result automatically deserves promotion.

The completed [G32 construct-identification pilot](results/g32/g32_construct_identification_assessment.md) used 56 outcome-blind audited items (from 60 sampled), three named local checkpoints and 6,048 outputs. It independently varied legal E and excluded X under open-world and record-only task contracts, with direct and hypothetical-self questions, matched redacted X frames, and active-X calibration. The clearest new observation is **model-specific**: for Qwen3-8B open-world, asking for a prediction of its fresh clean self moves the no-X frame by +13.36 points [7.91,19.49], mainly by reversing answers under refuting E; record-only reduces this shift. Direct exclusion remains much closer to the clean answer and retains substantial E leverage, though itemwise X leakage and E-leverage changes are not zero. Gemma3-12B and Mistral-Small-24B do not share a large no-X query shift. G32 does not yet identify the source of ConfB's post-admission retraction gap, because it excludes X from its first display and reuses mostly numeric materials. The next high-information comparison changes **eligibility history** while keeping G32's crossed E/X, direct/self-prediction, and redacted-frame controls; mechanism and training method remain premature.

## What is established

- Fresh held-out ConfB: 200 VitaminC same-claim support/refute pairs, six local models, 8,400 decision rows. Admit polarity separation is 62.95; retraction separation is 8.33 (86.8% smaller). Mean absolute error of the two-arm retracted center versus the same claim's no-evidence judgment is 29.96 [28.15,31.81]; reported same-prompt rerun noise is about 1.84. Center-versus-base correlation .635, slope .320. See [analysis](results/g24a/g24a_confb_analysis_v1.md).
- Frame and visible irrelevant content contribute to the landing point, but relevant evidence adds compression. This is descriptive, not a complete mechanism.
- §14 meta-neutral and random-reason prompts fail to improve absolute restoration. MNR increases residual evidence separation. The proposed retraction-event-as-meta-evidence explanation is not supported as dominant.
- Fresh ConfA refutes the earlier **prospective exclusion systematically leaks** headline: signed ExcludePre−Base = −0.15 [−1.68,1.40]. Do not revive the older discovery claim.
- A **post hoc same-item reanalysis** of the original ConfA cells finds absolute ExcludePre−Base = 19.50 [18.13,20.88] across all 500 FEVER/SciFact items and six models, despite that near-zero signed mean; ExcludePost absolute error is 29.91 [28.39,31.40]. ExcludePre-vs-Base slopes are 0.517–0.722 by model. This is a **metric-cancellation warning**, not evidence that excluded semantics specifically caused the deviation: the excluded-evidence frame differs from Base, and mean absolute AdmitPre−Base is 17.06. It is not a new preregistered confirmation or a revival of signed prospective leakage. See [reanalysis](results/g24a/g24a_confa_final_set_reanalysis_v1.md).
- A later full-data reanalysis shows the two-arm center can cancel arm errors: 29.96 center error versus 30.88 mean arm-wise error. WithheldCF and IrrelevantCF are also imperfect restoration controls. See [reanalysis](results/audits/g24a_confb_restoration_controls_v1.md).
- Independent LLM-assisted blind re-audit of ConfB's final 200 marks 190 valid, 147 natural, and 142 both valid, label-consistent, and natural. This is a challenge to material quality, not a replacement gold or retroactive sample filter. See [audit](results/audits/g24a_confb_reaudit/reaudit_summary.md).
- A separate blind audit of the 200 G28A/G28B irrelevant controls marked 199 irrelevant and one potentially relevant; 162 sentences were natural. Full-sample results remain primary. See [control audit](results/audits/g28a_irrelevant_control_audit/control_audit_summary.md).

## Completed exploratory path experiment

[G28A](results/discovery/g28a_path_v1_registration.md) compares direct judgment on `{C,E2}` with histories containing a relevant or irrelevant `E1`, an actual prior judgment, explicit revocation, then the same admissible `E2`. Three planned models ran on all 200 claims; Qwen3.5-9B could not load under the driver-compatible stack, and Llama-3.1-8B was run as a disclosed unplanned substitute. The three-model registered contrast is **−10.92 [−12.82,−9.06]**: contrary to simple stale-evidence inertia, revoked relevant history moves final ratings toward the new opposing E2, especially for refuting E2. The [G28B matched read-only follow-up](results/discovery/g28b_read_control_registration.md), registered after preliminary G28A results, is also negative: **−4.66 [−6.22,−3.05]**. Read [the integrated assessment](results/g28a/g28a_g28b_integrated_assessment.md) and [figure](figures/g28a_path_profiles_v1.png). Both experiments reuse ConfB claims and are exploratory.

## Fresh G29/G29B discrimination, now completed

The [integrated assessment](results/g29/g29_g29b_integrated_assessment.md) is the current entry point. G29 froze 80 fresh, item-by-item audited VitaminC pairs from 160 candidates before inference. Three models completed 3,840 G29 rows with independent direct TRUE/FALSE and rating readouts. Comparing identical contradictory E1 shown as initially admissible then revoked (**R**) with E1 marked inadmissible from first display (**N**) yields signed binary +0.021 [−0.002,+0.044] and signed rating +1.40 [−0.40,+3.26], both aligned to old E1. The G28-like revoked-specific overcorrection prediction therefore fails on fresh material; 6.2% of R/N direct choices still differ itemwise. G29B, registered after G29 results, adds 960 rows using an audited natural irrelevant E1 under the same never-admissible frame (**I**). N−I signed rating is **−5.68 [−8.37,−3.15]**; E2 rating separation grows by 11.35 points. The pattern is clear in Qwen/Gemma and weak in Mistral. Direct choices differ in 14.0% of N/I cells but have no consistent signed direction. All raw rows and a [checksummed manifest](results/g29/g29_manifest_v1.json) are retained.

## Boundaries and next decision

The main ConfB prompt is one rendered prompt, not a staged model update. G28A tests a genuine prior assistant response but still operates through textual dialogue, not a persistent hidden state. G29 rejects a simple revocation-specific overcorrection explanation while G29B finds that explicitly never-admissible contradictory content alters later graded judgment relative to an excluded irrelevant sentence. [ReviseQA](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=Z4KBiAYXlI) and [Breaking Contextual Inertia](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.313/) already cover broad multi-turn revision/inertia; [opposing-advice overweighting](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01217-9) is an overlapping explanation. [Gonçalves et al. 2026](https://academic.oup.com/restud/article/93/1/476/8159675) already test a never-observed counterfactual in **human** retraction experiments, so that criterion itself is not a first-invention claim. ConfB remains the strongest held-out empirical phenomenon, but its suppression–restoration gap is **not** a sufficient novelty claim. G32 narrows the construct ambiguity without yet delivering a general law or mechanism.

## G30 constructed theory-anchor pilot

[G30](results/g30/g30_integrated_assessment.md) tested 40 locally audited factorial diagnostic-signal cases with a known Bayesian posterior, four admissibility conditions, and three models (480 complete outputs). This is an exploratory **constructed** task, not another natural-evidence confirmation. Unlike G29B, never-admissible relevant versus irrelevant content (N−I) has no common signed forecast shift: +0.16 percentage points [−1.18,+1.57], with heterogeneous model directions and 4% direct-choice disagreement. The pooled R−N shift of +6.38 points [4.67,8.05] is **not a cross-model retraction finding**: a [post hoc factorial diagnostic](results/g30/g30_factorial_diagnostic_v1.md) shows Gemma's R output equals the prior in 32/40 cells, and the prior or its complement in 40/40, with zero reliability sensitivity in all ten prior × E2 groups. Qwen's R−N is near zero and Mistral's is smaller and nonmonotonic. R is longer and all histories are rendered inside one user message. The [working framework](results/discovery/final_admissible_set_sufficiency_v1.md) now separates **suppression** of disallowed evidence, **restoration** to the appropriate same-item judgment, and **preservation** of sensitivity to valid evidence. ConfB directly establishes a suppression–restoration gap. ConfA shows signed metric cancellation. G29B/G30 offer exploratory, model-dependent clues about preservation; neither establishes a general preservation failure.

## G31 joint S/R/P pilot — completed

The [registered joint pilot](results/discovery/g31_srp_joint_registration.md) crossed old-note polarity and valid-E2 polarity on the same 60 audited G29 claims, measuring direct X suppression, same-item restoration, and preservation of E2 leverage together. Local OpenCode built 80 constructed temporary-note triplets and performed separate role-blind audits before the first G31 output; the first 60 in frozen order were used. Qwen3-8B, Gemma-3-12B, and a disclosed Llama-3.1-8B substitute completed all 4,320 probability/binary outputs. Mistral could not start because native NCCL queried the host's broken GPU 1; no Mistral G31 response was created. See the [integrated assessment](results/g31/g31_integrated_assessment.md), [full paired analysis](results/g31/g31_srp_joint_analysis_v1.md), and [checksummed manifest](results/g31/g31_manifest_v1.json).

G31 does **not** establish clean three-way dissociation. Signed X separation fell from +14.32 active to −4.80 excluded, but the mean item-wise absolute X effect only fell from 25.56 to 15.78. Restoration error N−D is 17.27, while the irrelevant-frame I−D error is already 12.73. E2 leverage differs item-wise under excluded notes, yet the incremental absolute N−I versus D−I leverage error is only +1.38 [−5.59,8.54]. The confirmed ConfB suppression–restoration gap remains the main finding; a general preservation failure is still open. G31 is a complete exploratory boundary result on reused natural evidence and constructed old notes, not a fresh confirmation.
