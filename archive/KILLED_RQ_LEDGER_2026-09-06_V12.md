# 1. New V12 kill — CK current formulation

## CK — Finite Mutual Knowledge vs Genuine Common Knowledge

**KILL CURRENT FORMULATION — NON-OBVIOUSNESS / TEXTBOOK-CAPABILITY FAILURE.**

Core RQ:
> How do LLMs represent the qualitative boundary between finite mutual knowledge and genuine common knowledge?

Why killed:
- D2 PNAS 2026 data are excellent, but data quality cannot rescue question quality;
- the cleanest motivation reduces to a known theoretical distinction: finite mutual knowledge is not common knowledge;
- asking whether the model respects that distinction is a capability diagnostic;
- the interesting possibilities (overclosure, recognition/use dissociation, reasoning-model split) are possible post-hoc findings rather than an independently motivated pre-experimental tension;
- authorizing compute in the hope that one appears would reintroduce effect hunting.

Reviewer compression:
> “This is another higher-order ToM benchmark testing a textbook common-knowledge distinction.”

Do not resurrect by:
- adding deeper recursion;
- adding a coordination task after the fact;
- renaming overclosure;
- expanding the model zoo.

Return only if common knowledge becomes a substrate for a genuinely different, pre-motivated scientific question with different predictions.
