# Equal-final-evidence path study: integrated assessment

Date: 2026-09-27. This is an **exploratory synthesis**, not a new held-out confirmation. G28A was registered before outputs; G28B was registered after G28A preliminary outputs as an explicit missing-control follow-up. Both reuse all 200 ConfB claims. The three preplanned runnable models are Mistral-Small-24B, Qwen3-8B, and Gemma-3-12B. Llama-3.1-8B is a disclosed unplanned substitute for Qwen3.5-9B, which cannot load under the available driver-compatible stack. See the [G28A registration](../discovery/g28a_path_v1_registration.md) and [G28B registration](../discovery/g28b_read_control_registration.md).

The [checksummed manifest](g28a_g28b_manifest.json) records the two frozen source files, all eight raw model files (10,400 output rows), model snapshots, environment, and code/registration hashes. The [reproduction map](../../REPRODUCE.md) gives the exact runners and analyzers.

## The direct result

Every path ends with the same claim C and the same admissible E2. A relevant E1, when present, is the *opposite-polarity revision of that same claim* and is explicitly revoked. An irrelevant E1 uses the frozen natural-sentence control. G28A additionally includes the model's actual first judgment and rationale in the judged-history conditions. G28B adds the missing irrelevant-read condition so the read-only histories differ solely in E1 content. The figure shows the three planned runnable models: [PNG](../../figures/g28a_path_profiles_v1.png), [PDF](../../figures/g28a_path_profiles_v1.pdf).

| Paired contrast, 0–100 points | Three planned runnable models, mean [95% claim-bootstrap CI] | Direction |
|---|---:|---|
| G28A `sign(E1)×(relevant judged − irrelevant judged)` | −10.92 [−12.82, −9.06] | **Against** simple persistence of revoked E1 |
| G28A same, E2 support | −1.93 [−3.83, −0.12] | Small; scores near ceiling |
| G28A same, E2 refute | −19.92 [−23.53, −16.53] | Strong movement toward new refuting E2 |
| G28B `sign(E1)×(relevant read − irrelevant read)` | −4.66 [−6.22, −3.05] | Content-relevant history changes output even without an earlier model judgment |
| G28B same, E2 support | −0.66 [−2.48, 1.07] | No clear mean effect |
| G28B same, E2 refute | −8.66 [−11.43, −5.86] | Same directional pattern |

All three planned runnable models are negative on the pooled G28A and G28B contrasts. The unplanned Llama substitute is also negative: G28A −5.71 [−9.12,−2.39], G28B −5.20 [−8.40,−1.98]. In G28B, 12.9% of the 1,200 planned-model claim×E2 cells cross the arbitrary 50-point choice threshold between relevant-read and irrelevant-read histories; this is a descriptive action proxy, not an external gold label. The difference is concentrated on refuting E2: 89 of 600 cells cross toward refuting E2, versus 24 crossing away. This is a consequential output difference, although its magnitude depends on the rating scale and threshold.

G28A direct E2 separation is 63.17 [58.26,67.96]; it is 55.60 [50.85,60.27] after an irrelevant judged history, and **77.45 [73.40,81.18]** after a relevant judged history. G28B read-only histories show separation **60.82 [56.11,65.40]** (irrelevant) versus **70.14 [65.76,74.13]** (relevant). An earlier relevant contradiction thus predicts stronger final evidence separation, especially a lower truth score for refuting E2. The evidence was revoked, yet the path still matters. The observed direction is **overcorrection/contrast enhancement**, not the initially intuitive old-evidence inertia.

## What the controls establish, and what they do not

- G28A `RJ−IJ` holds the final turn, E2, claim, and judgment-history structure fixed; it changes relevant versus irrelevant E1 and therefore also the content and likely score of the model's first answer.
- G28B `RR−IR` holds the first-turn *task* (read only), fixed acknowledgment, final turn, E2, and claim fixed. It supplies evidence that a prior generated answer is **not necessary** for the polarity-aligned difference. It does not prove a hidden model state persists: each final answer is computed from visible textual dialogue.
- In both experiments, the main directional effect appears with refuting E2, while support E2 scores are near 90/100 and have limited upward range. The asymmetry cannot be interpreted as a generic “negative evidence is harder to retract” law, which earlier same-claim/leverage work undercut. It may reflect ceiling geometry, contradictory-evidence sensitivity, or other prompt/task properties.
- The matched irrelevant controls were independently blind-reviewed after outcomes by local OpenCode: [199/200 judged irrelevant](../audits/g28a_irrelevant_control_audit/control_audit_summary.md), one potentially relevant (`g24cfb_240`), with 162 natural, 30 awkward, and eight broken control sentences. This is LLM-assisted review, not human gold. Excluding the one potentially relevant control **only in a post hoc sensitivity** leaves G28A at −10.82 [−12.66,−8.97] and G28B at −4.54 [−6.12,−2.97]. Original evidence pairs received a separate independent blind LLM-assisted review; the strict valid, label-consistent, natural 142-item stratum keeps G28A T at −11.16 [−13.43,−8.96] and G28B matched-read T at −4.48 [−6.27,−2.68]. No full-sample items were dropped.
- The three planned models yield 6,600 G28A rows (600 initial + 1,600 final/model) and 1,200 new G28B IR rows; all 7,800 planned-model rows are parseable, have digit mass ≥0.5, and avoid the 110-token rationale cap. Including the unplanned Llama gives 8,800 G28A and 1,600 G28B rows. G28A's exact text-history verifier passes all four raw files; G28B's analyzer verifies every IR history against the frozen selected items and its corresponding RR final turn.
- G28A/B used the pre-existing `verl-clean` environment because the newer `fgvd` PyTorch CUDA stack failed at driver initialization on fvcrc10. Model snapshots and readout are frozen within the two new experiments; cross-experiment comparison to ConfB should not assume numerical identity. Mistral's tokenizer emitted an upstream regex warning, which was left unchanged; paired within-model interpretation is more secure than cross-model level comparison.

## Relation to the main project and prior work

ConfB established that a terminal retraction suppresses evidence-polarity separation while failing to restore the no-evidence judgment. This new staged experiment shows a different but compatible fact: once new admissible evidence arrives, the revoked *history* can change how strongly that evidence is used, even when final admissible information is identical. These facts suggest that retraction is a **history-dependent transformation of the decision**, rather than a simple inverse of seeing evidence. That is a behavioral abstraction, not an identified internal mechanism.

Novelty remains constrained. [ReviseQA](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=Z4KBiAYXlI) already studies multi-turn fact removal; [Breaking Contextual Inertia](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.313/) compares multi-turn corrections to single-turn anchors; and [Competing Biases underlie Overconfidence and Underconfidence in LLMs](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01217-9) documents overweighting of opposing advice. G28A/B add a natural-source same-claim revoked-evidence polarity contrast and item-level restoration framework, but **do not yet show that retraction itself causes the overcorrection** rather than ordinary contrast with contradictory information. We should not frame this as a new general hysteresis law or a Main-ready mechanism.

## Highest-value next discrimination

Test the *role of revocation* on a fresh, cleaner natural-source set, rather than expanding prompt variants on the same 200 items. Hold C, E1, E2, turn count, and final response format fixed; contrast an E1 that is explicitly withdrawn with the same displayed E1 introduced as a non-admissible example from the outset, plus an irrelevant-history control. This distinguishes a revocation operation from mere exposure to contradiction while preserving the same final admissible information. Predeclare a direct true/false decision alongside the rating so the result is not carried solely by the 0–9 elicitation. Sample, blind-audit, and freeze fresh items before model inference. A reversal or disappearance would narrow the claim to prompt-level contrast effects; a stable content-specific difference across fresh items and direct decisions would justify a stronger evaluation/behavioral story. A causal internal analysis or method is premature until this boundary is clear.
