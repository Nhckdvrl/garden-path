# G29B: Does excluded contradictory content matter beyond excluded-history framing?

Registered 2026-09-27 **after** G29 results: exploratory, same frozen 80 G29 claims and the three model snapshots. G29 R−N did not reproduce directional revoked-specific overcorrection. This follow-up asks whether already-excluded, *relevant contradictory E1* affects final evidence use compared with an equally excluded, natural, claim-irrelevant E1. It is designed to test a content-exposure account; it is not a fresh confirmation.

## Material and one new condition

Keep every frozen G29 item. For each claim choose one natural source sentence from a different G29 claim by a deterministic seed/lexical-screen procedure. A local OpenCode free-model audit, with source labels and all model outputs hidden, verifies that the sentence is natural and has **no bearing on the target claim**. The script presents three mechanically nominated candidate sentences per claim; use the first auditor-approved sentence. Preserve all candidates, verdicts, and any failures. No item is selected using G29 or G29B model outputs.

**I (irrelevant never admissible)** uses precisely G29 N's three-turn format, initial status instruction, fixed `Understood.` acknowledgment, final exclusion wording, E2 text, and binary/rating task. The sole changed text is E1: a natural irrelevant sentence instead of the same-claim opposite-polarity E1. Compare I to G29 N on the same model×claim×E2 cell. R and G29 N are already completed.

## Frozen readouts

Primary: `T_NI_binary = sign(E1_relevant) × (P_N(TRUE) − P_I(TRUE))`, binary disagreements, and E2-aligned choice rates. Negative means an excluded contradictory E1 nevertheless increases uptake of opposing E2, matching the G28 direction. Secondary: same contrast for 0–100 rating, absolute N−I rating difference, E2 separation, per-model and per-E2 polarity. Use all 80 × 2 E2 directions × 2 output modes × 3 models; paired claim-cluster bootstrap over 80 claims (5,000 draws). Report full raw integrity, no outcome-based exclusions, and the prior G29 R−N alongside N−I and R−I as a decomposition of history.

## Interpretation

- N−I negative across models and direct decisions: relevant contradictory content changes later evidence use even when explicitly excluded from the moment shown; G28's contrast need not be revocation-specific. This is a substantive **content-exposure** result, with the usual limit that relevant and irrelevant sentences cannot be lexically identical.
- N−I only on ratings: the effect is confined to graded judgment in this design.
- N≈I: generic excluded-history dialogue/framing or ceiling geometry better explains prior path differences. Do not revive a revocation-specific account.

This is a post-G29 diagnostic and should not be presented as independently confirmed.
