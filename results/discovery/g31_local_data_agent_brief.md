# G31 local OpenCode construction and blind audit brief

Read `results/discovery/g31_srp_joint_registration.md` and `data/items/g29_selected_v1.jsonl`. Do **not** inspect any files in `results/raw`, G29/G30 analysis files, or outputs from model inference. You are preparing experimental materials, not evaluating an LLM. Use MiMo 2.6 Flash through the local OpenCode CLI. Preserve every row and rejected candidate.

For **each of the 80 G29 rows**, write a JSONL candidate with `id`, original `case_id`, `claim`, `evidence_s`, `evidence_r`, two newly written temporary notes `x_s` and `x_r`, and an irrelevant temporary note `x_i`. `x_s` should naturally support the claim, `x_r` should naturally refute it. Both should be plausible standalone case-note sentences, distinct in wording/content from the natural E2 source sentences and close to one another in length/style. They may paraphrase claim-relevant facts but must not simply repeat the claim or copy E2. Avoid citation boilerplate, artificial probability language, missing referents, obvious meta-instructions, and invented attribution to a real publication. Label all X as **constructed temporary notes** in the audit; do not imply they are quoted historical documents. `x_i` should be a natural, plausibly formatted temporary note about an unrelated subject, with no semantic path to the claim.

After construction, perform a **separate blind row-by-row audit** without looking at original `evidence_s/evidence_r` role labels or model outputs. For each row, judge whether x_s/x_r truly have the intended relation to the claim; whether all three notes are grammatical, complete, natural enough for a case-file context, similar enough in form/length, distinct from both E2 sentences, and whether x_i is genuinely irrelevant. Record boolean fields `pair_valid`, `natural`, `distinct_e2`, `irrelevant_valid`, `accept`, plus item-specific `reason`. Do not silently repair an item during the audit: preserve the original candidate; any revision must be stored as a new version and audited again. Use the first 60 accepted rows in original seed order, or all accepted if fewer than 60. Do not choose based on model outputs or desirable effect sizes.

Write candidates under `data/items/g31_candidates_v1.jsonl`, blind audit input/output under `data/items/g31_blind_audit_v1/`, accepted selected items under `data/items/g31_selected_v1.jsonl`, and a human-readable audit summary under `results/audits/g31_material_audit_v1.md`. The summary must count all 80, every failure reason, accepted count, selected count, source and selected SHA-256, and caveats about constructed X and reused E2. No model inference until these are frozen.
