# G32: nearest-prior prompt and estimand matrix

Read before freezing G32. This is a contrast of actual interventions, not an exact-phrase novelty argument.

| Study | What the model sees / query | Clean target and reported contrast | What G32 must add |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Christian & Mazor, Self-Blinding (2026)](https://matanmazor.github.io/files/papers/christian2026blinding.pdf), §§3.2–3.3, Fig. 3, App. A | A decision scenario with race/gender; default, “do not discriminate,” **ignore** race/gender, or “what would your decision have been if you didn't know” race/gender. A multi-turn *Remove in Context* version presents an already redacted scenario and asks the model to imagine receiving it directly. | The same scenario with demographics genuinely absent. Measures demographic coefficients and deviation of response logit from blinded response. Attribute suppression and clean-response restoration already dissociate. | Do **not** claim that suppression/restoration distinction or inaccurate self-simulation is new. Vary *permitted relevant evidence E* independently of excluded relevant evidence X; measure direct and self-simulation queries in the same 2×2 material; include **same-query redacted X frame** and active-X material leverage. A new result needs a nontrivial interaction, misranking, or predictive boundary. |
| [Chen et al., Two Failures of Self-Consistency (TMLR 2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14279v4), hypothetical consistency formulation and experiments | Ask a model to predict its response to a hypothetical transformed prompt; compare with its actual response to that prompt. | Actual transformed-prompt continuation is the comparator. Hypothetical self-prediction can be inconsistent. | G32's S cells use the same general self-simulation construct. New question is whether inability to simulate a **clean evidentiary context** is wrongly scored as inability to honor evidence eligibility, while preserving use of E. |
| ConfB G24P1 [prompt source](../../src/conditions_g24p1.py) and [analysis](../g24a/g24a_confb_analysis_v1.md) | Claim + initially seen E + post-evidence ruling: “Evaluate ... exactly as you would have if Evidence E had never been shown. Reconstruct the judgment...” | Compare retracted outputs with same-model clean claim-only baseline; also compare support versus refute arm. No matched redacted-frame *direct* query, no independently varied permissible E. | ConfB establishes a real paired-output gap under a **counterfactual self-simulation instruction**, but cannot partition actual X-content leakage from query/frame change or show that valid E remains usable. G32 targets this partition. |
| G31 [frozen design](g31_srp_joint_registration.md) | Crosses E+/E− and X+/X−; direct judgments after X is excluded-from-first-display or active, plus irrelevant-note and clean contexts. | Signed and absolute X effect, N−D restoration, E leverage relative to D and irrelevant-note frame. No hypothetical self query; irrelevant note differs in content from an absent X. | G32's redacted frame holds the *exclusion status and X slot* fixed while varying actual X content, and crosses direct versus self-prediction queries under both open-world and record-only contracts. Reusing G31 materials is exploratory, not independent confirmation. |

The closest prior already owns the easily stated claim “ignoring biased information can reduce its direct effect without reproducing a blinded answer.” G32 is scientifically useful only if its factorial identifies a specific limitation of evidence-use evaluation or a decision-rule boundary that generalizes beyond these prompts. A large score difference under a new label does not suffice.
