# Literature diagnosis — 2026-09-28

Purpose: identify ownership of the actual question and decisive contrast, rather than keyword overlap. This is a research-positioning document, not a systematic-review claim. Companion: `research_diagnosis_2026_09_28.md`.

## Reading provenance and limits

The audit used primary PDFs/author copies and official publication pages. For the main competing papers, it inspected introduction, experimental interventions, comparators, main figures/tables and stated limitations. For the broader contextual papers, it inspected selected substantive sections; it does not claim every appendix or proof was independently verified. Downloaded reading copies are temporary, outside the repository. Links below are the durable references.

Two access gaps matter: OpenReview blocked the ReviseQA PDF despite both the paper URL and attachment endpoint being tried; its indexed first page and public author repository were inspected, not an accessible complete paper. The Garcia source-withdrawal preprint was available as an author abstract but its PDF was blocked. Neither is treated as full-text evidence for a detailed priority claim. Grounded Continuation was read from the currently available PDF, whose experiments include ReviseQA and MemoryAgentBench; old/version-specific descriptions should not be substituted for that version.

## Closest ownership: a more serious overlap than the unlearning slogan

**Christian & Mazor, Self-Blinding and Counterfactual Self-Simulation Mitigate Biases and Sycophancy in Large Language Models (2026).** [Author PDF](https://matanmazor.github.io/files/papers/christian2026blinding.pdf), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14553). Read §§1–7, Figures 2–7 and prompting details.

It varies race/gender while retaining decision scenarios, and compares each intervention with a genuinely blinded version. Figure 3 and §§3.2–3.3 explicitly distinguish smaller attribute effects from distance to the blinded response; in-context redaction can reduce the former while leaving large latter errors. Thus it owns a close version of our decisive S–R contrast, including decision scenarios and overall response shifts. ConfB adds strong paired evidence, six models and held-out confirmation, but those alone do not establish a new conceptual contribution. Its nonsignificant demographic coefficients do not prove itemwise equivalence. Its tool-based clean-context calls also preclude treating simple replay as a new method. Neither study yet identifies a general computation explaining the gap.

**Youssef et al., How to Make LLMs Forget: On Reversing In-Context Knowledge Edits (NAACL 2025).** [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.630/). Read problem formulation, §§4–5, results and limitations.

The target is the model's original output/distribution before a counterfactual in-context fact edit. Reversal tokens are learned with a clean-output KL target and an additional no-edit preservation term. This is prompt-level restoration, not merely parameter unlearning. Its decisive operation prioritizes parametric answers over editing context; selective retention of other useful contextual evidence is a different problem. A restoration LoRA or learned reset token would be especially weak without a new reason to need it.

**Chen et al., Two Failures of Self-Consistency in the Multi-Step Reasoning of LLMs (TMLR 2024).** [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14279v4). Read formal definitions and experimental comparisons.

Hypothetical consistency already asks whether a model can predict its own response in another prompt context; compositional consistency replaces intermediate questions with the model's own answers. This owns part of the parent construct behind “reconstruct what you would have said.” It does not by itself test selective evidence eligibility. It makes it essential to distinguish an evidence-use failure from a self-simulation failure rather than casually treating the two as identical.

## Human retraction and continued influence

**Steblay et al. (2006).** [Author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/6881940_The_Impact_on_Juror_Verdicts_of_Judicial_Instruction_to_Disregard_Inadmissible_Evidence_A_Meta-Analysis). Inspected research hypotheses, comparator definitions, moderator analyses, results and discussion.

The meta-analysis separates no-evidence controls, evidence without admonition and evidence ruled admissible. Instructions can mitigate effects without restoring never-exposed verdicts; timing and exclusion rationale matter. Therefore neither a no-exposure comparator nor incomplete restoration originated here. Its heterogeneous study comparisons are not same-model/item counterfactuals, and its results should not be reduced to universal backfire or a universal prospective disadvantage.

**Gonçalves, Libgober & Willis, Retractions: Updating from Complex Information (Review of Economic Studies 2026; online 2025).** [Paper](https://academic.oup.com/restud/article/93/1/476/8159675), [author manuscript](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10205481/1/Goncalves_rdaf032.pdf). Read design, Proposition 1, §§3–4 and main figures.

The important comparison is not just correction versus no correction. It compares retraction with never-observed histories and informationally matched fresh evidence. Random selection for verification controls what the retraction event itself conveys. The work studies diminished updating, complexity and updating after retractions. Thus these questions are not new in humans. Its intellectual contribution is identifying an operation-specific difference beyond ordinary updating distortions. A direct LLM replication needs an additional computational insight or a consequential contrast, not merely the same asymmetry.

**Lewandowsky et al., Misinformation and Its Correction (2012).** [Full author manuscript](https://skepticalscience.com/docs/Lewandowsky_2012_misinfo.pdf). Read the continued-influence paradigm, mental-model explanations and correction strategies.

Remembering/accepting a correction while still using the original information in subsequent inference is already central. Explanatory gaps and replacement accounts are old candidate mechanisms. This review is historical context, not sufficient evidence for a current universal backfire claim. Our models' information remaining available is intentional; persistence alone cannot establish our failure.

## Context interference and epistemic readout

| Primary source | Decisive intervention / comparator | Implication for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [From Evidence to Belief](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.531/), evidence taxonomy, main experiments and §8 | Evidence quality/type and source-strength cues against no-evidence answers; correctness and confidence | Lower confidence and poorer answers with irrelevant/conflicting evidence are known. ConfB must explain something beyond additional text changing confidence. |
| [How Is LLM Reasoning Distracted by Irrelevant Context?](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.674/), graph construction, main depth/distractor analyses and limitations | Controls reasoning depth, distractor count and graph connectivity | Even structural interactions between distraction and task complexity have been studied. “More distractors hurt” is not an adequate next route. |
| [LLMs can be easily Confused by Instructional Distractions](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.957/), benchmark construction, task examples and limitations | Requested transformation task versus instructions/questions embedded in its input | Text can change which task is executed. | [Llama See, Llama Do](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.791/), Figures 1–4, head intervention | Related, unrelated, random and counterfactual preceding tokens; answer-token logits; head ablation | Token carryover and a causal attention mechanism already exist. Locating “distractor heads” is not automatically an eligibility mechanism. |
| [The Distracting Effect](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.892/), §3, Figures 1–2 and RAG experiments | Retrieved/generated irrelevant passages; abstention and answer accuracy | Irrelevance is graded and retriever-dependent. One unrelated sentence is not a universal distributional placebo for relevant withdrawn evidence. |
| [Context Length Alone Hurts LLM Performance Despite Perfect Retrieval](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1264/), §§3–5 and main figures | Filler length, exact evidence retrieval, positional controls and fresh shorter prompts | Availability/access need not imply effective use. Simply re-showing the clean information inside the contaminated context is not equivalent to a fresh context. |

These works constrain explanations. They do not jointly “own” every selective-control question. A proposed contribution must say what additional distinction, intervention or explanatory finding survives their comparisons.

## Unlearning and editing: three meanings of restoration

| Primary source | Actual target | Novelty consequence |
|---|---|---|
| [Answer When Needed, Forget When Not](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1276/), method, Tables 2–4, logit-lens analyses | Train special unlearning tokens to emit `forgot` for designated knowledge while answering other queries | Information being decodable before late output suppression is occupied. Knowledge availability is not our failure criterion. |
| [The Mirage of Model Editing](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.745/), §§3–7 and Figure 3 | Replaces teacher-forced, target-length token matching with ordinary QA generation; separates evaluation modules | A strong evaluation contribution demonstrates a real mismatch in established practice, not just a more demanding score. It does not establish that all evidence-control papers use our signed metric. |
| [Revealing the Deceptiveness of Knowledge Editing](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.868/), attack probes, §§4.1–4.4 and limitations | Context attacks expose old answers after parameter editing; residual-stream and attention interventions | Superficial success plus old knowledge recovery and late attention effects is already a causal-mechanism story. Our intervention must explain restoration or policy-conditioned use specifically. |
| [Suppression or Deletion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18505), method, results and §6 | ViT class unlearning; SAE-selected activation restoration recovers forgotten behavior | Here “restoration” restores the forbidden information's effect. Our target restores the behavior without that evidence. The slogan overlaps; the decisive counterfactual is different. Do not cite it as direct LLM evidence. |
| [Unlearning as Distribution Restoration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19442), construction, oracle comparison, adversarial tests and §11 | Forget/retain acquisition, retraining reference and distributional rather than suppression-only checks | Strong ownership of the general evaluation principle. The paper itself limits its empirical certificates, small forget sets and oracle-free impossibility scope. It does not subsume prompt-level causal eligibility. |
| [CAP](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1882/), prompt generator, objectives, comparisons and limitations | Learned prefixes control a frozen target model; retained utility and reversible prompt removal | “Restoring” by removing the external control is distinct from undoing an exposed evidence item's effect inside a retained context. Prompt-controlled forgetting is nevertheless not new. |
| [Google's Machine Unlearning Challenge](https://research.google/blog/announcing-the-first-machine-unlearning-challenge/), full official post | Indistinguishability from retraining without the forget set plus utility | The clean counterfactual target is longstanding. Do not claim to invent it. |

## Agent relevance and downstream novelty

**[Revoked but Still Authoritative](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08258).** Read memory-system setup, measurement pipeline and guard. The intervention is storage/retrieval validity; the outcome includes agent actions. It demonstrates practical consequences but does not isolate our prompt-level exclusion computation. Conversely, merely adding an agent wrapper will not produce a new contribution.

**[Effective context engineering for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents).** Read the official article. Compaction, notes and separate contexts motivate systems relevance. This is engineering guidance, not a controlled demonstration of our phenomenon or its mechanism.

**[Grounded Continuation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14175).** Read dependency-map definitions, verifier guarantees, architecture and experiments. It already treats retraction as changing standing without deleting history, and propagates grounding through dependencies. “Maintain a source/dependency ledger and invalidate descendants” is not a new method proposal. Guarantees are conditional on the extracted representation; end-to-end interpretation remains a separate empirical component.

**[Which Number Is Sticky?](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7411618).** Abstract-only lead, full PDF blocked. It reports randomized anchors, withdrawn sources and replayed upstream valuations. This is enough to require full-paper checking before proposing “withdrawn evidence survives through the model's own earlier answer” as a new downstream line. It is not enough to certify all its controls or reject every downstream opportunity.

## What the taste anchors actually accomplished

| Paper, reading focus | Intellectual step | What should transfer to our work |
|---|---|---|
| [Language Models Resist Alignment](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1141/), compression model, theorem assumptions, forward/inverse alignment, scale/data tests, §6.1 | Turns apparent fragility into an explanatory quantity that supports additional directional predictions | A new explanation has to constrain unobserved behavior. Its idealized assumptions and incomplete lifecycle validation also show that strong work need not eliminate every alternative. |
| [A Theory of Response Sampling in LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1454/), controlled distributions/ideals, main figures, domain extension, §9 | Separates what a model knows about a distribution from what it samples; varies descriptive statistics and normative ideals independently | A new conceptual factorization can be the main idea. A neural mechanism is not required; identifying training origins is explicitly left open. |
| [Why are Sensitive Functions Hard for Transformers?](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.800/), sensitivity/sharpness connection, implications, experiments, limitations | Explains multiple difficulties with one computational property and separates representability from learnability | Seek an explanation whose consequences connect observations, not a new label for each failure. Its encoder/asymptotic scope should not be silently generalized to our decoder models. |
| [Mind the Value-Action Gap](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.154/), paired tasks, human validation, main tables and limitations | Builds credible operationalizations for concepts commonly conflated and shows substantial disagreement | Construct/measurement innovation can suffice. Its “actions” are scenario-based choices, not observed real-world deployment; our standard need not demand field trials. |
| [Measuring Chain of Thought Faithfulness by Unlearning Reasoning Steps](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.504/), intervention validity, controls, results and limitations | Changes the intervention so that a different causal construct becomes measurable | Research strategy itself can be the innovation. The imperfect unlearning intervention and alternate valid reasoning paths limit identification; the lesson is not “perform unlearning.” |
| [In-Context Learning with Long-Context Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.605/), selection, ordering, attention and finetuning comparisons, §8 | Studies interacting structural properties under one mother question; perturbations lead naturally to efficiency implications | A coherent empirical characterization can matter without a theorem or universal mechanism. Its task/model scope is bounded and acknowledged. |

The common standard is a new inference the reader can make after reading the paper. It can come from an idea, construct, perspective, intervention, mechanism, law or method. No one category is mandatory. More experiments, an untouched combination of keywords, or the absence of an exact dataset match is not that intellectual step.
