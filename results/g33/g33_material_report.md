# G33 material construction and source challenge

The selected 200 claim families have 200 distinct real Wikipedia-revision cases
and 200 distinct source pages. Every source hash and original source line is
retained. Historical claim/case pools were excluded before sampling; this is new
material relative to the project's earlier experiments, with one fixed format
smoke family. This is an exploratory identification study, not a held-out
confirmation of an already established new law.

Selection hash: `66bf60f5fe7a2fba12c698fb0adcabe4b8d93882be7f610b886fe045127e6e8c`.
The fixed selection boundary is `g33_0620`. Later unassessed candidates cannot
change the first 200 fully accepted families. Material and safeguards were
committed at `8e40c7f` before full inference.

The source release provides natural Wikipedia revision contrasts, including false
or vandalized versions. It does not guarantee logical refutation. We therefore
screened 22,190 mechanically eligible pairs, sampled a seeded 600-family prefix,
then appended 200 reserve families without changing the original prefix. At the
freeze, 720 candidates had completed construction and a different-model independent
audit; 365 were provisionally eligible. Of these, 347 received the extra source-only
challenge, 226 passed all applicable filters including manual exclusions, and 18
later eligible candidates were unassessed by that pass. The remaining 80 reserve
candidates lacked completed independent audits after provider failures. They are
not silently counted as semantic rejections. The first 200 fully accepted families
were selected without reading research-model outcomes.

This third pass was necessary: a seeded 16-item inspection of the tentative
selection exposed false refutations. A different sunniest city need not refute a
relative-sunlight claim; a different university does not exclude prior attendance;
using someone else's DNA in one experiment does not exclude the claimed person's
DNA in other experiments. Aliases, reputation claims, review-category overlap
and mangled acquisition syntax also required exclusion. Nine explicit manual
exclusions and the source-only challenge instructions are retained. The tentative
pre-challenge 200 are preserved but are not the study dataset.

Each final family has a faithful X+/X- summary pair, constructed from its natural
support/refute source versions. These are experimental paraphrases, **not** two
new independently acquired natural documents or actual quotations. E and X
polarity are crossed at rendering. Original page/topic metadata is shown with E
because source validation used that context to resolve pronouns. Surface cleanup
changes punctuation and whitespace; original source text is also preserved.
All currently admissible records are explicitly authoritative for the record-only
hypothetical task; factual correctness in today's world is not the gold label.

OpenCode's free MiMo, Longcat and Muse Spark models performed the row-level jobs.
Each canonical construction and first independent audit used different providers.
The source-only challenge is a separate blinded pass, but sometimes reuses one of
those providers; it is not represented as three independent expert annotators.
The manifest reports actual provider counts. LLM-assisted judgments remain fallible,
and this is not a human-adjudicated gold dataset. Extra cases, failed calls, late
attempts, all canonical judgments, and reasons are retained rather than erased.

There are no digit-bearing claims or artificial probability-threshold questions.
Natural spelled numbers/ordinals remain; dates and numbers in source evidence are
not removed. The experiment consequently does not depend on G32's predominantly
constructed numeric-threshold items.

Frozen policies, same-query frames and the binary readout are in the [registration](../../registrations/g33_final_identification_v1.md).
