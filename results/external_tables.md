# Source-grounded external boundary checks (pre-EVS-v1)

> **Provenance correction — 2026-08-29.** These experiments are useful external
> anchors, but they are not a fully untouched held-out dataset. The Ramsey task
> keeps the source task/instruction structure while generating new report values
> and sweeping fabricated-report offsets; the prospective condition is ours. The
> Baron-Hershey/Aiyer vignette text is source-grounded, but the explicit
> ALLOW/DENY policy sentences are our mapping into the controlled framework.
> Therefore these results are retained as **boundary checks**, not counted as the
> primary independently authored EVS-v1 confirmation tier. See
> `DATASET_REDESIGN.md` and `data/external/source_manifest.json`.

**A. Ramsey, Liu & Trueblood (2024)** medication-report paradigm, OSF 9ybnx.
The invalid-report instruction/task structure is grounded in their experiment.
Their design flags the fabricated report *in place*, which corresponds most
closely to the retrospective arm; announcing which future report will be
fabricated is our addition. The numeric instances below are generated by this
repository rather than copied verbatim from the original experiment, because the
original extreme outliers have too little Admit leverage on these models.

| model | n | base error | admit (unflagged) | flag in place (source-like) | flag announced first (ours) |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| qwen3-8b | 48 | +0.0 | +2.4 | **+0.0** | **+0.4** |
| gemma3-12b | 48 | -0.0 | +2.4 | **+0.1** | **+0.1** |
| mistral-small-24b | 48 | -0.0 | +1.6 | **+0.1** | **-0.0** |

Values are the signed pull toward the fabricated report, in patients per 100,
against the true mean of the truthful reports. 0 is perfect exclusion.

**Interpretation:** this source-grounded family is a strong **negative boundary
condition** for any claim that explicit invalidation generally fails in LLMs.
The tested models discard the flagged numeric report almost perfectly.

**B. Aiyer et al. (2023) replication of Baron & Hershey (1988)**, OSF knjhu.
The bypass-surgery vignette/decision/outcome text comes from their Qualtrics
material. The explicit rule that maps the outcome into an ALLOW/DENY condition
is ours. Four cells come from one semantic vignette, so this is an anchor rather
than an independent large-n LLM dataset.

| model | REI exclude-pre | REI exclude-post |
|---|---|---|
| qwen3-8b | +0.341 (n=4) | +0.435 |
| gemma3-12b | +0.600 (n=4) | +0.338 |

**Interpretation:** outcome information remains a promising true-but-temporally-
out-of-bounds family, but these four cells are not enough to validate the broad
claim. EVS-v1 should ingest legally reusable original materials with provenance
and count original semantic scenarios—not rendered cells—as independent units.
