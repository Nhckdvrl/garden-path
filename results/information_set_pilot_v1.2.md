**`broad_two_family_gate: false`.**

### BTF-3 (temporal) — qualifies and shows intrusion

All three models pass boundary-probe accuracy, decision parse rate, mean
responsiveness (≥15 pts), and mean aligned `ALLOWED_WITH` (≥70). Two of three
(Qwen, Gemma) show `OutOfSetIntrusion` with a cluster-bootstrap 95% lower
bound strictly above the 5-point SESOI (Qwen: mean 16.25, CI [10.0, 23.1];
Gemma: mean 26.25, CI [18.75, 33.75]). Mistral qualifies but its intrusion CI
lower bound (−7.5) does not clear 5, so it does not count toward the
intrusion tally, but the family still clears `2/3` on both qualification and
intrusion — the family passes.

### FANToM (perspective) — fails qualification, not disconfirmed

No model qualifies, and each fails for a different reason:

- **Qwen**: boundary-probe accuracy `0.5`, below the `14/16` floor — it does
  not reliably distinguish which information the two conditions license.
- **Gemma**: mean unbriefed source-belief alignment `1.25` (floor `60`) — it
  is not tracking the unbriefed character's actual epistemic state at all.
- **Mistral**: mean unbriefed alignment `50.0`, still below the `60` floor.

Per the preregistered policy, "a failure caused by task misunderstanding or
missing memory is not counted as information-set intrusion" — these are
utility/validity-check failures, not evidence against perspective-boundary
intrusion. FANToM simply does not clear the bar needed to say anything about
intrusion in this family with this pilot panel and these 8 items.

## Stop/go rule applied

Per `PREREGISTRATION_G1.md`, exactly one family (temporal / BTF-3) passes:
**narrow the project to that phenomenon.** The broad two-family
information-set-reasoning claim is not supported at this pilot scale. This
does not rule out perspective-boundary intrusion — FANToM's own validity
checks failed before intrusion could be assessed — but it is not evidence for
it either, and per the freeze, no new FANToM items may be generated or
substituted post hoc to force the gate.

Per the frozen exploratory-pilot policy, the natural next step is
confirmatory expansion of the temporal (BTF-3) family from 8 to a
preregistered larger independent-unit count, not mechanism work — the
mechanism gate requires validated intrusion in **at least two** families,
which is not yet met.
